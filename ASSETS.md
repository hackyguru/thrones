## Sound

Recorded effects are CC0 recordings from OpenGameArt, sliced and normalised by `scripts/blender/prep_audio.py`.
Music is generated live in the browser (`packages/client/src/audio/music.ts`): tanpura, sitar (Karplus–Strong synthesis)
improvising in Raag Yaman, tabla in teental and war drums. No recordings are used for it.

| In game | Source | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| sword hits and clashes (melee) | [20 sword sound effects](https://opengameart.org/content/20-sword-sound-effects-attacks-and-clashes) | StarNinjas | CC0 |
| swings, unsheathe, UI, cloth, drops | [RPG Sound Pack](https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack) | artisticdude | CC0 |
| chopping, mining, construction, coins, collapse | [68 workshop sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/68-workshop-sounds) | OpenGameArt contributor | CC0 |
